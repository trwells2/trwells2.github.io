Overview

The problem with paper.

The Fermilab Site Office is responsible for federal oversight of contractor safety performance at the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory. Inspections were conducted on paper: filled out by hand in the field, then re-entered by hand into a tracking system back at the office.

The process was slow and error-prone, and it left a meaningful share of findings unrecorded, or recorded too late to drive timely corrective action. Leadership needed something field staff would actually use, with no friction at the point of inspection.

PAR+ is that app. Every inspection now starts and ends in one place — findings, photographs, responsible parties and corrective actions — and the reporting layer reads the same records the inspectors write.

Process

From requirement to runway.

Six stages, in order. The prototype stage is the one that paid for itself.

  1. 01

    Requirements & discovery

    Interviewed contracting officers and inspectors to map the existing paper workflow step by step: what was being captured, what was being lost, and what the downstream consumers of that data actually needed. Field observations became a formal requirements document before any tooling was opened.

  2. 02

    UI prototyping in HTML

    Before building a single screen in Power Apps Studio, I prototyped the inspection form in HTML and CSS and walked end users through it. That validated information architecture and field sequencing cheaply, catching problems that would have been expensive to rework inside the app. Rework and post-release change requests dropped as a result.

  3. 03

    Data modelling in Dataverse

    The schema mirrors the real inspection hierarchy: site, inspection, finding, corrective action. Relationships, field types and business rules live at the data layer, so app logic stays clean and downstream reporting stays consistent.

  4. 04

    Canvas app build

    Built screen by screen from the validated prototype, following accessibility and usability standards. Power Fx drives the conditional logic — different inspection types surface different field sets — while the experience stays consistent whichever path a user takes. Photo capture and offline-aware patterns were in from the start.

  5. 05

    Automation & notifications

    Power Automate handles everything downstream of submission: routing findings to responsible parties, opening corrective action tracking, and notifying supervisors through HTML-formatted Outlook mail and Teams Adaptive Cards. The paper handoff disappeared entirely.

  6. 06

    Documentation & support

    Federal-standard documentation covering data definitions, flow logic and app administration, plus data quality checks and knowledge-transfer material for DOE recordkeeping and compliance requirements. The app has run in production for seven years with enhancements delivered throughout.
