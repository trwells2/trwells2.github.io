Overview

Scope, client and role.

The Fermilab Site Office is responsible for federal oversight of the contractor at the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory. The records that oversight runs on live in three places, SQL Server, Oracle and SharePoint Online, and reporting across them was siloed and manual.

I architected star-schema models in Power BI that unify all three, so a number means the same thing in every report, and built the dashboards on top with advanced DAX. Requirements come straight from the contracting officers who read them.

The production reports run on federal data and stay inside the agency. The Business Overview report below is a rebuild of one of them: the same two pages, measures and filters, over generated sample data.

Process

Six stages, question to dataset.

In order, from a contracting officer’s question to a governed dataset leadership can trust.

  1. 01

    Requirements from contracting officers

    Requirements come directly from the contracting officers and leadership who use the reports: the questions they ask, the measures that answer them, and the system each source record lives in.

  2. 02

    Connect the three sources

    SQL Server, Oracle and SharePoint Online are read where they already live. Power Query shapes them into consistent types and keys before anything is modeled, so a record means the same thing whichever system it came from.

  3. 03

    Model one star schema

    Fact tables for the records, shared dimensions such as date, category and status. Every page filters the same way, and every report reads the same model instead of its own export.

  4. 04

    Write the measures in DAX

    Received, completed, net flow, approval time and aging are defined once as DAX measures on the model, not as calculations inside a visual, so the overview and the workload pages always agree.

  5. 05

    Design for a quick read

    Headline tiles first, trends second, detail tables last, with one filter row that scopes everything on the page. Leadership gets the answer at a glance and the detail when they need it.

  6. 06

    Govern, document, hand over

    Governed datasets tuned for query performance and scale, data quality checks, and documentation and knowledge-transfer material that meet federal recordkeeping and compliance requirements.
