Overview

The problem with paper.

The DOE Fermilab Site Office is responsible for federal oversight of contractor safety performance at the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory. Inspections were conducted on paper forms — filled out by hand in the field, then manually re-entered into a tracking system back at the office.

The process was slow, error-prone, and left a meaningful portion of inspection findings unrecorded or recorded too late to drive timely corrective action. Leadership needed a solution that field staff would actually use, with zero friction at the point of inspection.

How it was built

From requirement to runway.

01

Requirements & discovery

Interviewed contracting officers and inspectors to map the existing paper workflow step by step. Documented what data was being captured, what was being lost, and what the downstream consumers of that data actually needed. Translated field observations into a formal requirements document before touching any tooling.

02

UI prototyping in HTML

Before building a single screen in Power Apps Studio, I prototyped the inspection form layout in HTML and CSS and walked end users through it. This let us validate information architecture and field sequencing cheaply — catching UX issues that would have been expensive to rework inside the app. Rework and post-release change requests dropped significantly as a result.

03

Data modeling in Dataverse

Designed the Dataverse schema to mirror the real-world inspection hierarchy: Site → Inspection → Finding → Corrective Action. Defined relationships, field types, and business rules at the data layer so the app logic stayed clean and reporting downstream stayed consistent.

04

Canvas app build

Built the Canvas app screen by screen, following accessibility and usability standards from the validated HTML prototype. Power Fx formulas drove conditional logic — different inspection types surfacing different field sets — while keeping the experience consistent regardless of path. Photo capture and offline-aware patterns were built in from the start.

05

Automation & notifications

Power Automate flows handle downstream actions on inspection submission: routing findings to responsible parties, triggering corrective action tracking, and notifying supervisors via HTML-formatted Outlook emails and Teams Adaptive Cards. The paper handoff was replaced entirely.

06

Documentation & ongoing support

Produced federal-standard documentation covering data definitions, flow logic, and app administration. Established data quality checks and knowledge-transfer materials to meet DOE recordkeeping and organizational compliance requirements. The app has been in active production use for 7+ years with iterative enhancements delivered throughout.
